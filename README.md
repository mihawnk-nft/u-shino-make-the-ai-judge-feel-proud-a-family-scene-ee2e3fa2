Entry for the [Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene](https://make.fun/c/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-proud-a-family-scene-ee2e3fa2) competition on make.fun.

## What it does

`main.py` writes one short family scene — two lines, warm, funny and heartfelt — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Every Sunday Dad sets an extra plate for Grandma — "in case she drops by," he says, like heaven is just down the road.
> Mum rolls her eyes, then quietly puts out Grandma's favourite cup.

The warmth is in the ritual (Dad still sets Grandma's place every Sunday), the humour in his wry excuse (heaven just down the road), and the heart in Mum rolling her eyes and then doing it too — putting out Grandma's favourite cup without a word.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Pure Python standard library, no network, no dependencies. The output is two lines of 185 characters (UTF-8), within the competition's 1–280 character gate.
